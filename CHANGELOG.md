# Changelog

All notable changes to Roots & Rivers are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [1.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added
- 22 chapters in four parts, each with a MicroSim, a 10-question quiz, references, a Whose Story? box and an Ask Your Elders interview guide.
- A 368-concept learning graph with an interactive viewer.
- Glossary, FAQ, and three appendices (pronunciation and scripts, country fact sheets, festival calendar 2026-27).
- Teacher guide, club kit, oral history project and story archive, impact page, pilot study kit.
- Printable PDFs of every chapter and the full book.
