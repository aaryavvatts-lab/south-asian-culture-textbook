# Changelog

All notable changes to Roots & Rivers are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [1.1] - 2026-10-22

### Added
- Interactive data charts (population by country, faiths by country, South Asian Americans) in Chapters 1, 5 and 21, Appendix B and a new Explore the Data page, each with a table view and source.
- How This Book Was Made, Get Involved and Contributors pages.
- Live counters on the Impact page, read from the repository's public issue forms.
- A dated roadmap through 2027.

### Changed
- A full specialist re-read of all 22 chapters: facts re-verified, out-of-date items updated to October 2026, fairness and wording fixes. The most important corrections are listed on the Accuracy and Corrections page.
- The About and Accuracy pages now describe plainly how the book was drafted and checked.
- Glossary, FAQ and appendices synced with the corrected chapters.

## [1.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added
- 22 chapters in four parts, each with a MicroSim, a 10-question quiz, references, a Whose Story? box and an Ask Your Elders interview guide.
- A 368-concept learning graph with an interactive viewer.
- Glossary, FAQ, and three appendices (pronunciation and scripts, country fact sheets, festival calendar 2026-27).
- Teacher guide, club kit, oral history project and story archive, impact page, pilot study kit.
- Printable PDFs of every chapter and the full book.
