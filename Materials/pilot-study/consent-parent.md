Dear parent or guardian,

Your child's [club or class] is using *Roots & Rivers*, a free online textbook about the cultures of South Asia. We are doing a small study to find out whether the book helps students learn. We are asking for your permission for your child to take part.

**What your child would do.** Your child would take a 20-question multiple-choice quiz and a short survey twice: once before the group starts Chapters 1 to 8 of the book, and once after. Each time takes about 20 minutes, during [club meetings or class time]. A copy of the quiz and the survey is attached so you can see every question.

**What we will not ask.** We will not ask about your child's ethnicity, religion, caste, immigration history or family. The survey asks only whether your child talked with someone about traditions recently, not what was said or with whom. Students do not need South Asian heritage to take part.

**It's voluntary.** Your child does not have to take part. Saying no will not affect grades, club membership or anything else. Your child will still take part in the lessons or meetings as usual; only the quiz and survey are optional. Your child can skip any question or stop at any time.

**No names.** Your child will use a code number instead of a name. Only [supervising adult] will have the list connecting names to codes, kept on paper in a locked place, and it will be destroyed once the before and after quizzes are matched. The quiz is not graded.

**Risks and benefits.** The risks are very small: the quiz may feel a little like a test, and it takes some time. Possible benefits are practice with the material and helping improve a free resource for other students.

**What we will share.** We will report results only as group totals and averages, for example in a science fair project, a school presentation or on the book's website. No one will be able to tell how any one student did. All paper and digital records will be destroyed by [date].

**Questions?** Contact [supervising adult] at [email or phone]. [If an IRB reviewed this study: For questions about your child's rights as a participant, contact [IRB contact].]
