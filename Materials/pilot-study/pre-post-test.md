**1.** Which of these is **not** one of the eight member countries of SAARC, the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation?

- A. Bhutan
- B. Myanmar
- C. the Maldives
- D. Afghanistan

**2.** In 2025, about how many people lived in the eight countries of South Asia, according to the World Bank?

- A. About 200 million, roughly one in forty people on Earth
- B. About 500 million, roughly one in sixteen people on Earth
- C. About 2 billion, roughly one in four people on Earth
- D. About 4 billion, roughly half the people on Earth

**3.** Mount Everest, on the border of Nepal and China, is about how tall, according to the 2020 joint China-Nepal measurement?

- A. 5,895 m
- B. 6,961 m
- C. 7,821 m
- D. 8,849 m

**4.** Which statement best describes the South Asian monsoon?

- A. A seasonal shift in winds that brings a rainy season
- B. Rain that falls every day of the year
- C. A type of earthquake common in the Himalaya
- D. A dry wind that blows only in winter

**5.** What is true about the writing found on Indus Valley (Harappan) seals?

- A. It was fully decoded in the 1920s.
- B. It is the same script as modern Hindi.
- C. Scholars have not yet deciphered it.
- D. It was written only on paper.

**6.** How do most scholars today describe the arrival of Indo-Aryan languages in South Asia?

- A. A single sudden invasion that destroyed the Indus cities
- B. A gradual migration over centuries, supported by linguistic and genetic evidence
- C. Languages brought by Portuguese sailors in the 1500s
- D. There is no evidence of any language change

**7.** In which year did the Mughal Empire begin with the First Battle of Panipat?

- A. 1206
- B. 1526
- C. 1757
- D. 1857

**8.** What is the Taj Mahal?

- A. A royal palace where emperors lived
- B. A fort built to defend Agra
- C. A mausoleum (tomb)
- D. A trading post of the East India Company

**9.** What was the British East India Company?

- A. The official government of Britain
- B. A trading company that came to rule large territories in South Asia
- C. A group of Indian merchants who opposed Britain
- D. A shipping company founded after 1947

**10.** On what dates did Pakistan and India mark their independence in 1947?

- A. Pakistan 14 August, India 15 August
- B. Both on 26 January
- C. Pakistan 15 August, India 14 August
- D. Both on 4 February

**11.** How did Bangladesh become an independent country?

- A. At Partition in 1947
- B. When Sri Lanka became independent in 1948
- C. After the Liberation War of 1971
- D. It has never been independent

**12.** When did Sri Lanka (then called Ceylon) become independent?

- A. 1857
- B. 1919
- C. 1948
- D. 1985

**13.** Who founded Sikhism?

- A. Guru Nanak
- B. Mahavira
- C. Ashoka
- D. Akbar

**14.** Which form of Buddhism is the main tradition in Bhutan?

- A. Theravada
- B. Zen
- C. Pure Land
- D. Vajrayana

**15.** How many languages are listed in the Eighth Schedule of India's Constitution?

- A. 2
- B. 22
- C. 50
- D. 122

**16.** Which statement about languages in India is correct?

- A. India has no national language; Hindi and English are official languages of the Union government.
- B. Hindi is India's only national language and is spoken by everyone.
- C. English is India's only official language.
- D. Sanskrit is the everyday language of most Indians.

**17.** Many South Asian scripts, such as Devanagari, Bengali, Tamil and Sinhala, descend from which ancient script?

- A. Latin
- B. Cyrillic
- C. Brahmi
- D. Chinese characters

**18.** Why does Eid move about 11 days earlier on the Gregorian (school) calendar each year?

- A. Because each country chooses a new date
- B. Because it follows a lunar calendar, which is shorter than the solar year
- C. Because it is always on the first Friday of spring
- D. Because of daylight saving time

**19.** Which statement about Diwali is accurate?

- A. Only Hindus mark Diwali.
- B. Diwali always falls on the same date each year.
- C. Diwali is a harvest festival from Sri Lanka only.
- D. Hindus, Sikhs and Jains all mark Diwali, each for their own reasons.

**20.** Chili peppers are used in many South Asian dishes today. Where did chili peppers originally come from?

- A. The Americas
- B. The Himalaya
