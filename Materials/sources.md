# Sources Policy

## Open sources we may adapt with attribution
- **US federal government works** (Library of Congress, Smithsonian where marked public domain, National Archives): public domain unless marked.
- **Wikipedia** (CC BY-SA 4.0): reference and lead-finding only, at most 3 per references page. Never copy text.
- **Wikimedia Commons public-domain images:** none are embedded in v1.0 (all visuals are drawn procedurally).

## Topic-map-only sources (copyrighted: never copy text, figures, or questions)
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, museum essays (The Met Heilbrunn Timeline, V&A, British Museum), Pew Research Center reports, UNESCO pages, academic books and articles, news articles. Facts may be used with citation; wording must be original.
- Song lyrics, poems and film scripts: never reproduce beyond one short attributed line.

## Preferred authorities by chapter
- **Geography and population (1, 17-20):** World Bank Open Data, UN DESA, national statistics offices (Census of India, Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, Sri Lanka Department of Census and Statistics, Nepal National Statistics Office, Bhutan NSB, Maldives Bureau of Statistics), NOAA, India Meteorological Department, Britannica.
- **History (2-4):** Britannica, The Met Heilbrunn Timeline, UK National Archives, British Library, Library of Congress, UNESCO World Heritage, The 1947 Partition Archive, Harappa.com, peer-reviewed history journals.
- **Faiths (5):** Pew Research Center, Harvard Pluralism Project, Britannica, academic religious studies sources.
- **Languages (6):** Constitution of India (Eighth Schedule), Unicode Consortium, census language tables, Britannica, UNESCO.
- **Festivals, food, arts, dress (7-12, 15):** UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage lists, Sangeet Natak Akademi, Ministry of Culture (India), Smithsonian, V&A, The Met, Britannica, timeanddate.com for dates, ICC for cricket, Academy Awards database.
- **Family and society (13):** Pew Research Center, national census household data, Constitution of India, peer-reviewed sociology.
- **Literature (14):** Nobel Prize, Poetry Foundation, Britannica, Library of Congress.
- **Science and ideas (16):** MacTutor History of Mathematics (University of St Andrews), Nobel Prize, Bodleian Libraries, NIH NCCIH (for Ayurveda and yoga safety).
- **Diaspora and identity (21-22):** SAADA, Pew Research Center, US Census Bureau, Minnesota State Demographic Center and Minnesota Compass, UK Office for National Statistics, Oral History Association, Library of Congress American Folklife Center.

## Never use
- Content farms, AI-generated sites, travel-agency blogs, pages without authors or dates, social media posts, partisan or communal advocacy sites on any side, forums.
