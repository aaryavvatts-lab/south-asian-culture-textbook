    Quiz: Cinema, Cricket, and Pop Culture¶

    Test your understanding of Chapter 15. Choose the best answer, then click Show Answer to check.

    Question 1¶

    What does "Bollywood" refer to?

    1. All films made in India
    2. All films made in South Asia
    3. The Hindi-language film industry based in Mumbai
    4. The Bengali film industry in Kolkata
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is C. Bollywood, a blend of "Bombay" and "Hollywood," is the Hindi-language industry in Mumbai. Using it for all Indian films (option A) is the most common mistake, since India also has big Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali and other industries.

    Concept Tested: Bollywood Bloom's Level: Remember

    Question 2¶

    In cricket, how many legal balls make up one over?

    1. 6
    2. 4
    3. 10
    4. 20
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is A. An over is 6 legal balls bowled by one bowler from one end. Twenty (option D) is the number of overs per side in a T20 match, not the number of balls in an over.

    Concept Tested: Cricket Bloom's Level: Remember

    Question 3¶

    Which Pakistani TV and online music series, launched in 2008, pairs artists from different regions and styles in live studio recordings?

    1. Humsafar
    2. Zindagi
    3. Joyland
    4. Coke Studio
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is D. Coke Studio Pakistan launched in 2008 and became the longest-running annual TV music show in Pakistan. Humsafar is a Pakistani drama, not a music show.

    Concept Tested: Coke Studio Bloom's Level: Remember

    Question 4¶

    Which statement best describes playback singing?

    1. Actors record their own songs live on set without music.
    2. Professional singers record songs that are played back while actors lip-sync on screen.
    3. Old songs are replayed at the end of a film's credits.
    4. Singers appear on screen next to the actors during song scenes.
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is B. Playback singers record the songs, and actors lip-sync to them; the singers usually don't appear on screen. Many, like Lata Mangeshkar and Noor Jehan, became as famous as the stars.

    Concept Tested: Playback Singing Bloom's Level: Understand

    Question 5¶

    Why are there two separate film industries working in the Bengali (Bangla) language?

    1. Bengali is spoken in both West Bengal, India, and Bangladesh, which became separate countries' film centers in Kolkata and Dhaka.
    2. One industry makes silent films and the other makes sound films.
    3. One industry makes films for children and the other for adults.
    4. The Dhaka industry only makes films in English.
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is A. The language is shared across the border, so Kolkata (India) and Dhaka (Bangladesh) each developed their own industry. Mukh O Mukhosh (1956) was the first Bengali feature made in what is now Bangladesh.

    Concept Tested: Regional Cinemas Bloom's Level: Understand

    Question 6¶

    What is a key reason the India-Pakistan cricket rivalry is so intense?

    1. The two teams play each other every week.
    2. It is the only international cricket either team plays.
    3. Politics and shared history shape it, and the teams now meet mostly at major tournaments.
    4. Only fans in the diaspora watch it.
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is C. The rivalry has been shaped by history and politics, including no matches from 1962 to 1977 and no bilateral series since a short one in 2012 to 2013, so rare tournament meetings carry huge weight. Option A is wrong: they meet rarely, which is part of what makes each match special.

    Concept Tested: India-Pakistan Rivalry Bloom's Level: Understand

    Question 7¶

    In one over, a bowler bowls: 1, dot, 4, wide, 2, wicket, 6. How many runs did the batting team score, and how many legal balls were bowled?

    1. 13 runs, 7 legal balls
    2. 14 runs, 6 legal balls
    3. 13 runs, 6 legal balls
    4. 14 runs, 7 legal balls
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is B. The runs are 1 + 0 + 4 + 1 (the wide) + 2 + 0 + 6 = 14, and the wide does not count as a legal ball, so there are 6 legal balls. Option D counts the wide as a legal ball, which is the most common error.

    Concept Tested: Cricket Bloom's Level: Apply

    Question 8¶

    Ben wants to watch a South Asian film that is not from Bollywood, made outside India, and nominated for an Oscar. Using the chapter, which film fits best?

    1. Alam Ara (1931)
    2. RRR (2022)
    3. Pather Panchali (1955)
    4. Lunana: A Yak in the Classroom (2019)
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is D. Lunana is a Bhutanese film in Dzongkha that was nominated for Best International Feature Film at the 2022 Oscars. RRR (option B) won an Oscar for its song, but it is an Indian Telugu film, so it doesn't meet the "outside India" condition.

    Concept Tested: Global South Asian Pop Bloom's Level: Apply

    Question 9¶

    Hank Azaria stopped voicing Apu on The Simpsons in 2020, and shows like Never Have I Ever and Ms. Marvel were created with South Asian writers. What does this comparison suggest about representation in media?

    1. Representation is now fully solved.
    2. South Asian characters no longer appear on American TV.
    3. Only awards matter for measuring representation.
    4. Who creates and voices a character has become an important part of how South Asians are shown.
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is D. The shift from a stereotyped character voiced by a white actor to characters created by South Asian writers shows that who tells the story matters. Option A goes too far, since many communities still rarely appear on screen.

    Concept Tested: Representation in Media Bloom's Level: Analyze

    Question 10¶

    Compare cricket and field hockey in South Asia. Which statement is best supported by the chapter?

    1. India and Pakistan dominated Olympic field hockey for decades, while cricket's biggest titles have been won by India, Pakistan and Sri Lanka.
    2. Field hockey is more popular than cricket in every South Asian country.
    3. Neither India nor Pakistan has ever won an Olympic hockey gold.
    4. Only India has won cricket World Cups among South Asian countries.
    Show Answer

    The correct answer is A. India won eight Olympic hockey golds and Pakistan three, while India, Pakistan and Sri Lanka have all won the men's Cricket World Cup. Option D ignores Pakistan's 1992 and Sri Lanka's 1996 titles.

    Concept Tested: Field Hockey Bloom's Level: Analyze
