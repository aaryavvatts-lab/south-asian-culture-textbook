#### Question 1

What happened at the Battle of Plassey in 1757?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. The East India Company defeated the Nawab of Bengal, helped by the betrayal of the Nawab's commander
2. Indian soldiers at Meerut rose up against the East India Company
3. The British Crown took over the government of India from the East India Company
4. Britain fought its first war in Afghanistan
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. At Plassey, Robert Clive's Company forces defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah after Mir Jafar kept his troops out of the fight, giving the Company control of Bengal. The Meerut uprising (B) was the start of the Revolt of 1857, a century later.

    **Concept Tested:** Battle of Plassey
    **Bloom's Level:** Remember

#### Question 2

Which statement best describes the princely states?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Provinces governed directly by British governors
2. Independent countries outside the British Empire, like Nepal
3. Kingdoms with their own Indian rulers who accepted British control of defense and foreign affairs
4. Territories created by the Radcliffe Line in 1947
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **C**. About 565 princely states kept their own rulers but were tied to Britain by treaties. They were not directly ruled provinces (A), and unlike Nepal they were inside the British system (B).

    **Concept Tested:** Princely States
    **Bloom's Level:** Remember

#### Question 3

Why is the Revolt of 1857 called by different names, such as the "Sepoy Mutiny" and the "First War of Independence"?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Because historians disagree about the year it took place
2. Because each name reflects a different view of who rebelled and why
3. Because it happened in two separate countries
4. Because the British Crown renamed it in 1858
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **B**. "Sepoy Mutiny" treats it as a soldiers' revolt, while "First War of Independence" treats it as the start of a national struggle. The date (A) is not in dispute: it began on 10 May 1857.

    **Concept Tested:** Revolt of 1857
    **Bloom's Level:** Understand

#### Question 4

Which pair of dates gives Pakistan's and India's independence days?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. 26 January 1950 and 15 August 1947
2. 4 February 1948 and 14 August 1947
3. 23 March 1940 and 26 March 1971
4. 14 August 1947 and 15 August 1947
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **D**. Pakistan celebrates 14 August and India 15 August 1947. 26 January 1950 (A) is when India's Constitution took effect, and 4 February 1948 (B) is Sri Lanka's independence day.

    **Concept Tested:** Partition of 1947
    **Bloom's Level:** Remember

#### Question 5

What made satyagraha different from other ways of resisting British rule?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. It relied on secret armed attacks against officials
2. People openly and nonviolently broke unjust laws and accepted the punishment
3. It asked Britain to divide India into two countries
4. It was a petition sent only by lawyers to Parliament
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **B**. Satyagraha, "holding firmly to truth," meant open, nonviolent refusal to obey unjust laws, as in the Salt March. Armed attacks (A) were the path of some revolutionaries, not Gandhi.

    **Concept Tested:** Satyagraha
    **Bloom's Level:** Understand

#### Question 6

Why did the timing of the Radcliffe Line's publication add to the confusion of 1947?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. It was published two days after independence, so many people did not yet know which country they were in
2. It was published a year before independence, giving people too long to argue
3. It was never published, so the border was decided by the armies
4. It was published only in English newspapers in London
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. The Line was announced on 17 August 1947, after both countries had become independent, so many people celebrated without knowing where the border would fall. It was published (C), and it came after independence, not before (B).

    **Concept Tested:** Radcliffe Line
    **Bloom's Level:** Understand

#### Question 7

Zara's Nanu says, "I was born in Pakistan in 1960, near Chattogram." Which explanation is correct?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. She is mistaken, because Chattogram has always been in India
2. She is mistaken, because Bangladesh was created by Partition in 1947
3. She is right only if she later moved to Lahore
4. She is right, because from 1947 to 1971 Chattogram was in East Pakistan
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **D**. Chattogram was in East Pakistan from 1947 until Bangladesh's independence in 1971. Option B repeats a common myth: Partition created India and Pakistan, and Bangladesh came later, in 1971.

    **Concept Tested:** Bangladesh Liberation War
    **Bloom's Level:** Apply

#### Question 8

A student uses the MicroSim and notices that between 1948 and 1971 one block only changes its name. Which block is it, and what does that show?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. India; it shows India changed its borders in 1971
2. Ceylon; it shows Sri Lanka was partitioned
3. East Pakistan becoming Bangladesh; it shows a new country can form while the land and people stay in place
4. West Pakistan becoming Afghanistan; it shows the Anglo-Afghan Wars changed borders
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **C**. The East Pakistan block keeps its size and place and becomes Bangladesh, showing that the country changed around people who never moved. Ceylon (B) was never partitioned. It changed its name to Sri Lanka in 1972.

#### Question 9

Compare the Partition of 1947 with Sri Lankan Independence in 1948. Which is the best explanation of why Partition was far more violent?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Partition divided mixed provinces like Punjab and Bengal in a few weeks, while Ceylon became independent as one unit after years of negotiation
2. Ceylon had no British officials, so there was nothing to fight over
3. Partition happened during the Second World War, while Sri Lanka's independence came after it
4. Sri Lanka was a princely state, so its ruler kept order
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. Splitting mixed provinces at high speed, with borders announced after independence, set off mass migration and violence, while Ceylon was handed over whole. Option C is wrong because Partition took place in 1947, after the war had ended.

    **Concept Tested:** Sri Lankan Independence
    **Bloom's Level:** Analyze

#### Question 10

Why do historians give Partition migration as a range, about 14 million to 18 million people, instead of one exact number?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Because the British government kept exact records but has not released them
2. Because only people who crossed by train were counted
3. Because Pakistan and India agreed to use different numbers
4. Because no one kept a full count, and different methods, such as refugee records and census comparisons, give different totals
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **D**. Refugee counts and census-based estimates produce different totals, and a census study found about 3.4 million people unaccounted for. There is no hidden exact record (A). The gaps come from the chaos of 1947.

    **Concept Tested:** Partition Migration
    **Bloom's Level:** Analyze
