# References: Festivals Through the Year

1. [Holidays to be Observed in Central Government Offices During the Year 2026](https://www.gconnect.in/news/holidays-to-be-observed-in-central-government-offices-during-the-year-2026.html) - Government of India, Department of Personnel and Training (reproduced by Gconnect) - The official 2026 holiday list used for this chapter's dates, including Holi, Eid al-Fitr, Buddha Purnima, Dussehra and Diwali, with the matching dates in India's national Saka calendar.

2. [Change in the date of holiday on account of Id-u-Zuha (Bakrid) to 28th May, 2026](https://www.staffnews.in/2026/05/change-in-the-date-of-holiday-on-account-of-id-u-zuha-bakrid.html) - Government of India, Department of Personnel and Training order of 22 May 2026 (reproduced by StaffNews) - A real example of a holiday moved by one day after the moon was sighted.

3. [Durga Puja in Kolkata](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/durga-puja-in-kolkata-00703) - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage - Describes the ten days of Durga Puja, from clay sculptures shaped by local artisans to the final immersion, and why UNESCO inscribed it in 2021.

4. [Garba of Gujarat](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/garba-of-gujarat-01962) - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage - Explains the circle dance performed during Navaratri in Gujarat, inscribed in 2023.

5. [Mangal Shobhajatra on Pahela Baishakh](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/mangal-shobhajatra-on-pahela-baishakh-01091) - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage - The story of the Bengali New Year procession in Dhaka, begun by Fine Art students in 1989 and inscribed in 2016.

6. [Nawrouz, Novruz, Nowrouz ... (Nowruz)](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/nawrouz-novruz-nowrouz-nowrouz-nawrouz-nauryz-nooruz-nowruz-navruz-nevruz-nowruz-navruz-01161) - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage - The shared listing of Nowruz across many countries, including Afghanistan, India and Pakistan.

7. [Vesak Day](https://www.un.org/en/observances/vesak-day) - United Nations - Explains what Vesak commemorates and how the UN General Assembly recognized the Day of Vesak in 1999.

8. [H. Res. 824: Recognizing the religious and historical significance of the festival of Diwali](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hres824ih/html/BILLS-119hres824ih.htm) - US Government Publishing Office - A 2025 House resolution that sums up what Diwali means to Hindus, Sikhs and Jains, and why it matters to South Asian Americans.

9. [Diwali becomes a California state holiday](https://spectrumnews1.com/ca/la/politics/2025/10/20/diwali-state-holiday-california-newsom) - Spectrum News 1 - Reports on AB 268, signed in October 2025, which makes Diwali a California state holiday from 2026, and notes Pennsylvania and Connecticut acted first.

10. [Boycotting Dashain: History, Memory and Ethnic Politics in Nepal](https://nepalica.hadw-bw.de/nepal/books/viewitem/723) - Susan Hangen, *Studies in Nepali History and Society* (2005) - A scholarly study of why some Indigenous groups in Nepal stopped celebrating Dashain from the 1990s.
