# References: Food and the Table

1. [Eight-in-ten Indians limit meat in their diets, and four-in-ten consider themselves vegetarian](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2021/07/08/eight-in-ten-indians-limit-meat-in-their-diets-and-four-in-ten-consider-themselves-vegetarian/) - Pew Research Center (2021) - The source for this chapter's vegetarianism numbers, from a survey of nearly 30,000 Indian adults. It breaks the results down by religion and explains rules about beef, pork and root vegetables.

2. [For people from South Asia, mornings are often powered by chai](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/23/nx-s1-5935277/for-people-from-south-asia-mornings-are-often-powered-by-chai) - NPR, Diaa Hadid (2026) - A radio story reported from Karachi on how British tea companies marketed tea to South Asians in the 1930s and how people turned it into milky, spiced chai.

3. [Chai as a Colonial Creation: The British Empire's Cultivation of Tea as a Popular Taste and Habit Among South Asians](https://hdl.handle.net/1794/28059) - Nayantara Arora, *Oregon Undergraduate Research Journal* 21(1) - A student research article, free to read, that uses plantation records and old advertisements to show how tea drinking was promoted in colonial India. A good model for your own history research.

4. [Chili: Small Fruit Sets Global Palettes on Fire](https://archive-yaleglobal.yale.edu/chili-small-fruit-sets-global-palettes-fire) - YaleGlobal Online, Yale University (2004) - Traces the chili pepper from the Americas around the world, including the Portuguese role in bringing it to India.

5. [International Year of Millets 2023](https://www.fao.org/millets-2023/en) - Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations - Explains why the UN named 2023 the Year of Millets and why these hardy grains matter for dry regions and a changing climate.

6. [Inside the world's largest free kitchen at Punjab's Golden Temple, feeding 100,000 daily](https://www.thenationalnews.com/weekend/2023/10/13/india-sikhism-sikh-golden-temple-gurudwara/) - The National, Taniya Dutta (2023) - A look inside the langar at the Golden Temple in Amritsar: the volunteers, the vegetarian menu and the rule that everyone eats together.

7. [Columbian exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbian_exchange) - Wikipedia - An overview of the plants, animals and diseases that moved between the Americas and the rest of the world after 1492, with links to Alfred Crosby's original work.

8. [Thali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thali) - Wikipedia - Describes thali meals in different regions, the katori bowls, and related meals such as the banana-leaf feasts of South India. Follow its citations to regional cookbooks and food histories.

9. [Rasgulla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rasgulla) - Wikipedia - Lays out the competing Odisha and Bengal histories of the rasgulla and the 2017 and 2019 Geographical Indication decisions discussed in the Whose Story? box.
