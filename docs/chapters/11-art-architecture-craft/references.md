# References: Art, Architecture, and Craft

1. [Significance of Kolam in Tamil Culture](https://www.sahapedia.org/significance-of-kolam-tamil-culture) - Sahapedia - A clear introduction to kolam as a daily women's art in Tamil Nadu: rice flour, the grid of dots (pulli), how the lines are drawn, why the designs are meant to disappear, and the names of related floor arts in other regions.

2. [Array Grammars and Kolam](https://doi.org/10.1016/0146-664x(74)90011-2) - Gift Siromoney, Rani Siromoney and Kamala Krithivasan, *Computer Graphics and Image Processing* 3 (1974): 63-82 - The paper that described kolam patterns with formal drawing rules a computer can follow. Advanced reading, but a good example of art and computer science coming together.

3. [Rickshaws and rickshaw painting in Dhaka](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/rickshaws-and-rickshaw-painting-in-dhaka-01589) - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage - Describes the painted rickshaws of Dhaka, how the craft is taught in workshops, and why it was added to UNESCO's Representative List in 2023.

4. [Masterpieces to Go: The Trucks of Pakistan](https://archive.aramcoworld.com/issue/200502/masterpieces.to.go.the.trucks.of.pakistan.htm) - AramcoWorld (2005) - A magazine feature on Karachi's truck painters: what they paint, how the decorations are made, and how a Pakistani truck ended up in the Smithsonian.

5. [Painted Truck (The Silk Road, 2002)](https://festival.si.edu/2002/the-silk-road/painted-truck/smithsonian) - Smithsonian Folklife Festival - Photos and notes from 2002, when Karachi artists Haider Ali and Jamil Uddin decorated a truck on the National Mall.

6. [Tibetan Thangka Painting (Sacred Pictures)](https://education.asianart.org/resources/tibetan-thangka-painting-sacred-pictures/) - Asian Art Museum of San Francisco - A teacher-friendly guide to what thangkas are, how they are made and used, and what their images mean.

7. [The Politics of Mithila Painting](https://orias.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/the_politics_of_mithila_painting_2-2017.pdf) - David L. Szanton, ORIAS, University of California, Berkeley - A short scholarly essay on how women's wall paintings in Mithila moved onto paper during the drought of the late 1960s and became an art known worldwide.

8. [Temporary Tattoos, Henna/Mehndi, and "Black Henna": Fact Sheet](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/temporary-tattoos-hennamehndi-and-black-henna-fact-sheet) - US Food and Drug Administration - Explains the difference between natural henna and "black henna," and why PPD in black henna can cause serious skin reactions.

9. [Mughal painting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_painting) - Wikipedia - An overview of Mughal miniature painting from Humayun to Shah Jahan, including the Hamzanama, court workshops and famous artists, with links to museum collections.

10. [Hindu temple architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_temple_architecture) - Wikipedia - Explains the parts of a Hindu temple (sanctum, tower, hall, gateways) and the differences between the Nagara and Dravida styles, with many examples.
