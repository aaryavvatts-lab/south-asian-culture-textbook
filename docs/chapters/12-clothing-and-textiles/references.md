# References: Clothing and Textiles

1. [Traditional art of Jamdani weaving](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-art-of-jamdani-weaving-00879) - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage - The official 2013 listing for Jamdani, Bangladesh's hand-woven cotton. It explains the discontinuous weft technique, how weavers around Dhaka pass the skill from parent to child, and why Bengali women treat the Jamdani saree as a symbol of identity.

2. [First Evidence of Cotton at Neolithic Mehrgarh, Pakistan](https://hal-mnhn.archives-ouvertes.fr/mnhn-02997043) - Christophe Moulherat, Margareta Tengberg, Jérôme-F. Haquet and Benoît Mille, *Journal of Archaeological Science* 29 (2002) - The original study of the mineralized cotton threads in a copper bead from a Neolithic burial at Mehrgarh, dated to the sixth millennium BCE.

3. [Sericulture (Statistical Year Book, Chapter 11)](https://www.mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/Statistical_year_book_india_chapters/SERICULTURE-WRITEUP_0.pdf) - Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Government of India - A two-page government summary of India's silk industry that defines mulberry, tasar, eri and muga silk and explains which states produce each.

4. [Khadi and Village Industries Commission](https://www.kvic.gov.in/kvicres/index.php) - Government of India - The official site of the body that has supported khadi since 1957. It describes khadi as hand-spun, hand-woven cloth tied to the swadeshi spirit of the freedom struggle, and explains the Khadi mark.

5. [Sikh Identity](https://www.sikhcoalition.org/about-sikhs/identity/) - The Sikh Coalition - A Sikh civil rights organization explains the meaning of the dastar (turban) and uncut hair, and notes that both men and women may wear a turban. Useful before asking a friend about their turban.

6. [Royal Textile Academy of Bhutan](https://www.rtabhutan.org/) - Royal Textile Academy of Bhutan - The home page of the academy founded in 2005 in Thimphu to preserve Bhutanese weaving, with its weaving school, textile museum and conservation center.

7. [Wool Creatives Nepal: Making All the Right Connections](https://enhancedif.org/en/impact-story/wool-creatives-nepal-making-all-right-connections-key) - Enhanced Integrated Framework (a multilateral trade program) - A 2018 story about Nepal's chyangra goats and how the Chyangra Pashmina brand was built to mark genuine Nepali pashmina.

8. [Driglam namzha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driglam_namzha) - Wikipedia - Background on Bhutan's code of etiquette and dress, its 17th-century roots, the 1989 rule making it mandatory, and the debate it caused among Lhotshampa in the south. Follow its citations for Bhutanese sources.

9. [Ajrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajrak) - Wikipedia - An overview of Sindh's block-printed cloth: carved wooden blocks, resist dyeing and natural dyes led by indigo, the names of its patterns, and its role in Sindhi identity.

10. [Dhaka muslin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka_muslin) - Wikipedia - The history of Bengal's "woven air," from Mughal courts to its collapse under colonial rule and recent revival efforts in Bangladesh.
