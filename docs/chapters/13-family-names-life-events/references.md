# References: Family, Names, and Life Events

1. [Attitudes about caste](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2021/06/29/attitudes-about-caste/) - Pew Research Center (2021) - A survey chapter on how Indians of every religion identify with caste categories, how they see discrimination, and how strongly many want marriages to stay within caste. A good source of real numbers.
2. [Religion in India: Tolerance and Segregation](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2021/06/29/religion-in-india-tolerance-and-segregation/) - Pew Research Center (2021) - The overview of the nearly 30,000-person survey behind the caste figures, with context on religion and family life in India.
3. [How Indians View Gender Roles in Families and Society](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2022/03/02/how-indians-view-gender-roles-in-families-and-society/) - Pew Research Center (2022) - Survey results on who should earn money, care for children and make decisions at home. Useful for the "Gender Roles and Change" section.
4. [Religion and household makeup around the world](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2019/12/12/religion-and-living-arrangements-around-the-world/) - Pew Research Center (2019) - Compares household types and sizes around the world, including large extended-family households in Afghanistan and Pakistan.
5. [Article 17: Abolition of Untouchability](https://www.constitutionofindia.net/articles/article-17-abolition-of-untouchability/) - Constitution of India (constitutionofindia.net) - The text of the article that abolished untouchability. Read it alongside Article 15 on the same site.
6. [Universal Declaration of Human Rights](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights) - United Nations - Article 16 says marriage requires the free and full consent of both people, which is the line that separates arranged from forced marriage.
7. [Sikhism: Weddings](https://www.bbc.co.uk/religion/religions/sikhism/ritesrituals/weddings.shtml) - BBC Religions (archived) - A clear step-by-step description of the Anand Karaj, including the four laavan and the Anand Marriage Act of 1909.
8. [Islam: Weddings](https://www.bbc.co.uk/religion/religions/islam/ritesrituals/weddings_1.shtml) - BBC Religions (archived) - Explains the nikah, the mahr and why the couple's consent is required in Islamic marriage.
9. [Suggested Explanations of Sheepa (Sherpa) Names](https://www.himalayanclub.org/hj/20/19/suggested-explanations-of-sheepa-names/) - The Himalayan Journal, vol. 20 (1957) - An older journal note listing the Sherpa names tied to each day of the week, such as Nyima (Sunday) and Dawa (Monday).
10. [Kinship terminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinship_terminology) - Wikipedia - A starting point for comparing how languages sort relatives, including the Dravidian system used in Tamil and Sinhala. Follow its citations to anthropology sources.
