# References: Cinema, Cricket, and Pop Culture

1. [The 95th Academy Awards (2023)](https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/2023) - Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences - The official list of 2023 winners, including "Naatu Naatu" from RRR (Original Song) and The Elephant Whisperers (Documentary Short Film).
2. [The 81st Academy Awards (2009)](https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/2009) - Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences - The official record of Slumdog Millionaire's eight Oscars, including A. R. Rahman's two wins and Resul Pookutty's award for Sound Mixing.
3. [Awards](https://satyajitray.org/awards/) - Satyajit Ray Society (satyajitray.org) - A list of the honors Satyajit Ray received, including his 1992 Oscar for lifetime achievement. The rest of the site has his films and writings.
4. [Law 17: The Over](https://www.lords.org/mcc/the-laws/the-over) - Marylebone Cricket Club (MCC), keeper of the Laws of Cricket - The official rule that an over is 6 valid balls bowled from alternate ends. The neighboring pages explain wides, the pitch and boundaries.
5. [Best Global Music Performance, 2022](https://www.grammy.com/awards/categories/best-global-music-performance/2022/) - Recording Academy (GRAMMY.com) - The official category page showing Arooj Aftab's win for "Mohabbat."
6. [Olympic Field Hockey history: Records, past winners, best moments, year-by-year results](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/field-hockey-101-olympic-history-records-and-results) - NBC Olympics - Year-by-year Olympic hockey results, showing India's eight and Pakistan's three men's gold medals.
7. [List of Feature Films Certified in 2023](https://cbfcindia.gov.in/cbfcAdmin/assets/pdf/Films_certified_2023.pdf) - Central Board of Film Certification, Government of India - The official list of films certified in 2023, sorted by language. Scroll through it to see how many languages Indian films are made in.
8. [On this day: Bangladesh gained Test status in 2000](https://www.tbsnews.net/sports/day-bangladesh-gained-test-status-2000-266497) - The Business Standard (Bangladesh) - A short history of how Bangladesh became the tenth Test-playing nation.
9. [Cinema of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_Pakistan) - Wikipedia - An overview of Pakistani film from Lahore's Lollywood to the Karachi revival. Use its footnotes to find newspaper and academic sources.
10. [India-Pakistan cricket rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India%E2%80%93Pakistan_cricket_rivalry) - Wikipedia - A detailed history of the rivalry, including the 1962 to 1977 gap and cricket diplomacy. Follow its citations for match reports.
