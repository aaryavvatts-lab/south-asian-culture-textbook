# References: Science, Math, and Ideas

1. [Zero](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/HistTopics/Zero/) - MacTutor History of Mathematics, University of St Andrews - A careful history of zero as a placeholder and as a number, from Babylon and the Maya to Brahmagupta, Baghdad and Fibonacci. Read it to see why historians say no single person "invented" zero.

2. [Indian numerals](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/HistTopics/Indian_numerals/) - MacTutor History of Mathematics, University of St Andrews - Traces the digits from Brahmi numerals to place value, including the earliest dated documents and the Gwalior inscription of 876 CE.

3. [Arabic numerals](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/HistTopics/Arabic_numerals/) - MacTutor History of Mathematics, University of St Andrews - Follows the digits from South Asia through Baghdad, al-Khwarizmi and North Africa to Europe, with a clear timeline of dates.

4. [Madhava of Sangamagrama](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Madhava/) - MacTutor History of Mathematics, University of St Andrews - A biography of the founder of the Kerala School, with his infinite series for pi, sine and cosine, and an honest discussion of what is and isn't known.

5. [Carbon dating finds Bakhshali manuscript contains oldest recorded origins of the symbol 'zero'](https://www.glam.ox.ac.uk/node/218481) - Bodleian Libraries, University of Oxford (2017) - The original 2017 announcement about the Bakhshali manuscript. Read it alongside the 2024 report below to see how the claim changed.

6. [Radiocarbon dating of the Bakhshālī manuscript](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:5a6d1dd7-f20c-4209-adb6-33849f5b08f4) - Chivall, Lladó-Buisán, Howell and Evison, Oxford University Research Archive (2024) - The revised radiocarbon results for five leaves, dating them to roughly the late 700s to early 1000s CE. A real example of scientists correcting an earlier result.

7. [Ayurvedic Medicine: In Depth](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/ayurvedic-medicine-in-depth) - National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NIH) - What Ayurveda is, what research has and hasn't shown, and why some products can be unsafe. A reliable starting point before believing any health claim.

8. [Yoga: Effectiveness and Safety](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/yoga-effectiveness-and-safety) - National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NIH) - Summarizes what research says yoga may help with, where the evidence is weak, and how to practice safely.

9. [Sir C. V. Raman: Facts](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1930/raman/facts/) - The Nobel Prize - Official facts about Raman's 1930 Nobel Prize in Physics, his birthplace and his work on the scattering of light.

10. [An ancient wind-powered iron smelting technology in Sri Lanka](https://www.nature.com/articles/379060a0) - Gill Juleff, *Nature* 379 (1996) - The research paper (abstract free to read) that showed how ancient Sri Lankan furnaces used monsoon winds to make high-carbon steel.
