# References: India

1. [India State/UTs & Districts](https://www.india.gov.in/explore-india/facts-of-india/states-ut-districts) - National Portal of India, Government of India - The official list of India's 28 states and 8 union territories with their capitals, updated in 2026. Use it to check any state or capital in this chapter.
2. [Article 1: Name and Territory of the Union](https://www.constitutionofindia.net/articles/article-1-name-and-territory-of-the-union/) - Constitution of India (constitutionofindia.net) - The opening article that calls the country "India, that is Bharat," with notes on how the Constituent Assembly debated the name.
3. [Eighth Schedule: Languages](https://www.constitutionofindia.net/schedules/languages) - Constitution of India (constitutionofindia.net) - The list of India's 22 scheduled languages and how it grew from 14 in 1949. Helpful for the sections on linguistic states and Hindi.
4. [Overall 65.79 percent voter turnout recorded in General Elections 2024, says ECI](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/overall-65-79-percent-voter-turnout-recorded-in-general-elections-2024-says-eci) - All India Radio News (Prasar Bharati) - The Election Commission's turnout figures for the 2024 general election, including the highest and lowest states.
5. [President Murmu promulgates Ladakh Official Languages Regulation 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/president-murmu-promulgates-ladakh-official-languages-regulation-2025) - All India Radio News (Prasar Bharati) - A short report on the 2025 rule that made Bhoti and Purgi official in Ladakh, a good example of how India's language map keeps changing.
6. [Chandrayaan-3 Details](https://www.isro.gov.in/Chandrayaan3_Details.html) - Indian Space Research Organisation - ISRO's own description of the Chandrayaan-3 lander, rover and science instruments.
7. [India: Quick Facts](https://www.britannica.com/facts/India) - Encyclopaedia Britannica - Current basic facts about India, including its government, leaders, languages and currency.
8. [Population, total: India](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?locations=IN) - World Bank Open Data - The source of this book's 2025 population figure for India. Switch to "Urban population (% of total)" to see how fast India's cities are growing.
9. [Hindi rises, speakers of South Indian languages and Urdu fall, shows 2011 census data](https://scroll.in/article/884754/surging-hindi-shrinking-south-indian-languages-nine-charts-that-explain-the-2011-language-census) - Scroll.in - Charts that explain the 2011 census language data, including the share of Indians who named Hindi, Bengali and other languages as their mother tongue.
10. [Adivasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adivasi) - Wikipedia - An overview of India's Adivasi communities, the meaning of the word, the major groups and regions, and the 2011 census share (8.6%). Use it as a starting point and follow its census citations.
