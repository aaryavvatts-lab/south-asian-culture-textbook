# References: Bangladesh and Sri Lanka

1. [The Sundarbans](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/798) - UNESCO World Heritage Centre - The official World Heritage listing for Bangladesh's part of the Sundarbans (1997), describing the mangroves, tidal waterways, tigers and birds of the delta coast.
2. [Sacred City of Anuradhapura](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/200) - UNESCO World Heritage Centre - A short official description of Sri Lanka's ancient capital and its sacred Bodhi tree, with photos and maps.
3. [International Mother Language Day](https://www.unesco.org/en/days/mother-language) - UNESCO - Explains why the world marks 21 February and how the day connects to protecting languages everywhere, a good follow-up to Ekushey February.
4. [The Nobel Peace Prize 2006](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2006/summary/) - NobelPrize.org - The official prize page for Muhammad Yunus and Grameen Bank, with links to their lectures on microfinance.
5. [Floods in Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floods_in_Bangladesh) - Wikipedia - An overview of why Bangladesh floods, how much land floods in an average year, and the great floods of 1987, 1988 and 1998. Follow its citations to the original studies.
6. [UN: Sri Lanka mass deaths may be 'war crimes'](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2011/4/25/un-sri-lanka-mass-deaths-may-be-war-crimes) - Al Jazeera (2011) - News report on the UN panel of experts' findings about the final months of the civil war, including the government's response.
7. [Tarique Rahman sworn in as new Bangladesh prime minister](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/2/17/incoming-bangladesh-pm-tarique-rahman-lawmakers-sworn-into-parliament) - Al Jazeera (17 February 2026) - Report on the swearing-in after the February 2026 election and the July Charter referendum.
8. [OHCHR report on 2024 protests in Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OHCHR_report_on_2024_protests_in_Bangladesh) - Wikipedia - A summary of the UN human rights fact-finding report on July and August 2024, its estimate of deaths and the responses to it, with links to the report itself.
9. [IMF Executive Board Completes the Combined Fifth and Sixth Reviews Under the Extended Fund Facility for Sri Lanka](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/en/node/20395) - Central Bank of Sri Lanka (2026) - The official notice on Sri Lanka's IMF program in 2026, useful for understanding the recovery after the 2022 crisis and Cyclone Ditwah.
10. [Population, total: Bangladesh and Sri Lanka](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?locations=BD-LK) - World Bank Open Data - The source of this book's 2025 population figures for both countries, with a chart going back to 1960.
