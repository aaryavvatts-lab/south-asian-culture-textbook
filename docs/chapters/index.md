# List of Chapters

Each chapter has the chapter itself, a 10-question quiz and a list of references. Start anywhere: each chapter lists what you should read first.

## Part 1: Foundations

1. **[What Is South Asia?](01-what-is-south-asia/index.md)**: Meet the eight countries of South Asia and the mountains, rivers, plains, seas and monsoon that shape where about 2 billion people live. ([Quiz](01-what-is-south-asia/quiz.md) · [References](01-what-is-south-asia/references.md))
2. **[Ancient Roots](02-ancient-roots/index.md)**: From the first farmers at Mehrgarh to the Indus cities, the Vedas, the Buddha, Ashoka and the Guptas: South Asia from about 7000 BCE to 550 CE. ([Quiz](02-ancient-roots/quiz.md) · [References](02-ancient-roots/references.md))
3. **[Empires and Encounters](03-empires-and-encounters/index.md)**: Trace the kingdoms, empires, faiths and traders of South Asia from about 600 to 1800, and see how many powers lived side by side. ([Quiz](03-empires-and-encounters/quiz.md) · [References](03-empires-and-encounters/references.md))
4. **[Colonialism, Independence, and Partition](04-colonialism-and-partition/index.md)**: How British rule spread across South Asia, how people organized for freedom, and how one colony became India, Pakistan and Bangladesh. ([Quiz](04-colonialism-and-partition/quiz.md) · [References](04-colonialism-and-partition/references.md))
5. **[Faiths of South Asia](05-faiths-of-south-asia/index.md)**: Meet the faiths of South Asia, from Hinduism, Islam and Buddhism to Sikhism, Jainism, Christianity, Parsis and Jewish communities, and compare them fairly. ([Quiz](05-faiths-of-south-asia/quiz.md) · [References](05-faiths-of-south-asia/references.md))
6. **[Languages and Scripts](06-languages-and-scripts/index.md)**: Meet South Asia's language families and scripts, learn how an abugida works, and see why Hindi, Bangla and Tamil letters are cousins. ([Quiz](06-languages-and-scripts/quiz.md) · [References](06-languages-and-scripts/references.md))

## Part 2: Living Culture

7. **[Festivals Through the Year](07-festivals/index.md)**: Why Diwali, Eid, Vaisakhi and other South Asian festivals land when they do: solar, lunar and lunisolar calendars, and how each festival is celebrated. ([Quiz](07-festivals/quiz.md) · [References](07-festivals/references.md))
8. **[Food and the Table](08-food-and-the-table/index.md)**: Explore how grain, climate, trade and faith shape what people eat across South Asia, from the thali and dal to biryani, chai, street food and hospitality. ([Quiz](08-food-and-the-table/quiz.md) · [References](08-food-and-the-table/references.md))
9. **[Music](09-music/index.md)**: Learn how raga and tala work, compare Hindustani and Carnatic music, and hear qawwali, ghazal, Baul, folk, bhangra and film songs across South Asia. ([Quiz](09-music/quiz.md) · [References](09-music/references.md))
10. **[Dance and Theater](10-dance-and-theater/index.md)**: Meet South Asia's classical dances, folk dances, theater and shadow puppets, and learn the hand gestures and emotions that let dancers tell stories. ([Quiz](10-dance-and-theater/quiz.md) · [References](10-dance-and-theater/references.md))
11. **[Art, Architecture, and Craft](11-art-architecture-craft/index.md)**: Stupas, temples, Mughal tombs, miniature paintings, thangkas, kolam, mehndi, block prints, truck art and rickshaw art across South Asia. ([Quiz](11-art-architecture-craft/quiz.md) · [References](11-art-architecture-craft/references.md))
12. **[Clothing and Textiles](12-clothing-and-textiles/index.md)**: Cotton, silk and pashmina, the weaves and prints of eight countries, and the sarees, kurtas, ghos, turbans and bangles people wear today. ([Quiz](12-clothing-and-textiles/quiz.md) · [References](12-clothing-and-textiles/references.md))
13. **[Family, Names, and Life Events](13-family-names-life-events/index.md)**: Kinship words, joint families, names, caste and the law, and the rituals that mark birth, growing up, marriage and death across South Asia. ([Quiz](13-family-names-life-events/quiz.md) · [References](13-family-names-life-events/references.md))
14. **[Stories and Literature](14-stories-and-literature/index.md)**: Epics, fables, poets and novels from across South Asia, and how the same stories travel and change across languages, faiths and borders. ([Quiz](14-stories-and-literature/quiz.md) · [References](14-stories-and-literature/references.md))
15. **[Cinema, Cricket, and Pop Culture](15-cinema-cricket-pop-culture/index.md)**: Bollywood and the many other film industries of South Asia, playback singers, dramas, Coke Studio, global stars, and cricket, hockey, kabaddi and carrom. ([Quiz](15-cinema-cricket-pop-culture/quiz.md) · [References](15-cinema-cricket-pop-culture/references.md))
16. **[Science, Math, and Ideas](16-science-math-ideas/index.md)**: How zero and place value grew in South Asia and traveled the world, plus grammar, healing traditions, chess, steel and modern scientists. ([Quiz](16-science-math-ideas/quiz.md) · [References](16-science-math-ideas/references.md))

## Part 3: The Eight Countries

17. **[India](17-india/index.md)**: Tour the Republic of India: its constitution, elections, 28 states and 8 union territories, languages, peoples, symbols, economy and space program. ([Quiz](17-india/quiz.md) · [References](17-india/references.md))
18. **[Pakistan and Afghanistan](18-pakistan-and-afghanistan/index.md)**: Tour Pakistan's provinces and languages and Afghanistan's land, peoples and heritage, and follow the Khyber road that has linked them for centuries. ([Quiz](18-pakistan-and-afghanistan/quiz.md) · [References](18-pakistan-and-afghanistan/references.md))
19. **[Bangladesh and Sri Lanka](19-bangladesh-and-sri-lanka/index.md)**: Tour Bangladesh's river delta, mangroves, language movement and garment economy, and Sri Lanka's island heritage, peoples, tea and recent history. ([Quiz](19-bangladesh-and-sri-lanka/quiz.md) · [References](19-bangladesh-and-sri-lanka/references.md))
20. **[Nepal, Bhutan, and the Maldives](20-nepal-bhutan-maldives/index.md)**: Tour three small South Asian nations, from Everest and Himalayan dzongs to coral atolls barely above the sea, and see how altitude and water shape life. ([Quiz](20-nepal-bhutan-maldives/quiz.md) · [References](20-nepal-bhutan-maldives/references.md))

## Part 4: Here and Now

21. **[The South Asian Diaspora](21-the-diaspora/index.md)**: Follow South Asians to the Caribbean, East Africa, Britain, the Gulf and the US, including Minnesota, and learn why each wave of migration happened. ([Quiz](21-the-diaspora/quiz.md) · [References](21-the-diaspora/references.md))
22. **[Bring It Home](22-bring-it-home/index.md)**: Identity, stereotypes, appreciation and allyship, then the oral history method and how a student club can turn stories into community action. ([Quiz](22-bring-it-home/quiz.md) · [References](22-bring-it-home/references.md))
