# Contact

The best way to reach the project is through GitHub. You don't need to be a programmer; the forms are short.

- **Found a mistake?** [Report an error](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=1-error-report.yml). Corrections are listed on the [Accuracy](accuracy.md) page.
- **Something feels unfair or one-sided?** [Request a sensitivity review](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=5-sensitivity-review.yml). We take these seriously.
- **Used the book in a class or club?** [Tell us](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=2-classroom-use.yml). It helps us know who the book is reaching.
- **Have an idea?** [Suggest it](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=3-suggestion.yml).
- **Teach or study South Asia professionally?** [Volunteer as an expert reviewer](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=4-expert-review.yml) for a chapter.

For the EVHS South Asian Student Association, see [evhs-sasa.vercel.app](https://evhs-sasa.vercel.app/) or Instagram [@evhs.sasa](https://www.instagram.com/evhs.sasa/).
