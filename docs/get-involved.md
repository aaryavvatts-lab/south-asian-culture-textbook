# Get Involved

Everyone who helps with Roots & Rivers is credited by name on the [Contributors](contributors.md) page (or anonymously, if they prefer).

## For students

- **Record pronunciations.** Version 1.0 uses written respellings only, because computer-made audio can get a word wrong. If you speak Bangla, Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Nepali, Sinhala, Pashto, Dari, Dzongkha or Dhivehi, you can record the key words from a chapter on your phone. [Suggest a recording](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=3-suggestion.yml) and we'll send you the word list.
- **Translate a chapter summary** into a language your family speaks, so grandparents can read along.
- **Share a story** from an Ask Your Elders interview with the [Story Archive](oral-history.md), with permission.
- **Start a club session** with the [Club Kit](club-kit.md) at your own school, then [tell us how it went](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=2-classroom-use.yml).

## For families and community members

- **Be interviewed.** If a student asks you the Ask Your Elders questions, your answers are what the book's interview guides are built around.
- **Tell us if something is wrong or unfair** about your community, faith or language. Use the [sensitivity review form](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=5-sensitivity-review.yml).

## For teachers

- **Try one lesson** from the [Teacher Guide](teachers.md) and [report your classroom use](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=2-classroom-use.yml). Those reports decide what changes in the next version.
- **Run the pilot study** with your class (it is consent-first and confidential); the kit is in the repository under `Materials/pilot-study/`.

## For scholars

- **Review one chapter.** It takes about 30 minutes. [Volunteer as a reviewer](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=4-expert-review.yml); with your permission, you'll be credited on the [Accuracy](accuracy.md) page.

## For developers

The book is open source. Fix a typo, improve a MicroSim, or suggest a new one on [GitHub](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook). See `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository.
