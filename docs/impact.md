## What we will measure

| Question | How we'll measure it |
|---|---|
| Are people reading it? | Page views, if site analytics are turned on (no personal data) |
| Who is using it? | Reports through the ["I used this book" form](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/south-asian-culture-textbook/issues/new?template=2-classroom-use.yml) |
| Do readers learn? | Pre and post quizzes in South Asian Student Association meetings, using the pilot study kit (with consent) |
| Do readers talk with their families? | Number of Ask Your Elders interviews reported in club sessions and stories shared in the [Story Archive](oral-history.md) |
| Is it accurate? | Number of errors reported and fixed, on the [Accuracy](accuracy.md) page |

## The pilot

The first planned pilot is with the South Asian Student Association at Eastview High School in Apple Valley, Minnesota, during the 2026-27 school year. Students who agree to take part (with parent permission) take a short quiz before and after a set of chapters. Results will be reported here only as group totals, with no names.
