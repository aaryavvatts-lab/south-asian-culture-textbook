# Roadmap

## Version 1.0 (October 2026)

22 chapters, 22 MicroSims, a 368-concept learning graph, a glossary, an FAQ, three appendices, a teacher guide, a club kit, an oral history project and printable PDFs.

## Version 1.1 (planned)

- **Real voices.** Pronunciation recordings by real speakers (SASA members and families, with permission) for the key words in each chapter. Version 1.0 uses written respellings only, on purpose: we would rather have no audio than robot audio that gets a word wrong.
- **First stories in the Story Archive**, shared with consent through the [Oral History Project](oral-history.md).
- **Expert review** of at least one chapter in each part by a teacher or scholar of South Asia, credited on the [Accuracy](accuracy.md) page.
- **Pilot results** from SASA meetings, using the pre/post quiz kit, posted on the [Impact](impact.md) page.

## Later

- Translations of the chapter summaries into Bangla, Hindi, Nepali, Sinhala, Tamil and Urdu, done by volunteer speakers.
- More MicroSims, such as a raga explorer and a monsoon-and-crops model.
- A teacher's question bank aligned to Minnesota's ethnic studies standards.
