# Roadmap

These plans will change as readers, teachers and clubs report what they need. Each milestone below says how its result will be checked.

## Done

- **Version 1.0 (October 2026).** 22 chapters, 22 MicroSims, a 368-concept learning graph, glossary, FAQ, three appendices, teacher guide, club kit, oral history project and printable PDFs.
- **Version 1.1 (October 2026).** A full specialist re-read of every chapter, interactive data charts, the [Explore the Data](data.md) page, [How This Book Was Made](methods.md), [Get Involved](get-involved.md), [Contributors](contributors.md), and live counters on the [Impact](impact.md) page.

## Next (2026-27 school year)

| When | Milestone | How we'll know it worked |
|---|---|---|
| Fall 2026 | First Club Kit sessions with the EVHS South Asian Student Association | Sessions run; feedback logged on the Impact page |
| Fall 2026 | Pilot study with SASA members who agree to take part, using the pre and post quiz | Group results posted on the Impact page, with sample size and limits |
| Winter 2026-27 | First expert review of a chapter by a teacher or scholar of South Asia | Reviewer credited on the Accuracy page; corrections logged |
| Winter 2026-27 | First stories in the [Story Archive](oral-history.md), shared with consent | Stories published with first names only |
| Spring 2027 | **Version 1.2:** pronunciation recordings by real speakers for each chapter's key words | Audio added, speakers credited on Contributors |
| Spring 2027 | Chapter summaries translated into Bangla, Hindi, Nepali, Sinhala, Tamil and Urdu by volunteer speakers | Translations published and credited |

## Later

- **Version 2.0:** more MicroSims (a raga explorer, a monsoon-and-crops model), a teacher question bank aligned to Minnesota's ethnic studies standards, and changes driven by pilot results.
- Other schools' South Asian clubs using the Club Kit, listed on the Contributors page.

Have an idea, or want to help with one of these? See [Get Involved](get-involved.md).
